How it works

Four steps from idea to finished ring.

There’s no add-to-cart button here, and that’s on purpose. Every commission starts with a conversation.

Tell me the idea

Send me the fandom, the palette, the vibe, or the reference photos. Rough and half-formed is completely fine — that’s the normal starting point.

Get a quote

Because nothing here is pre-made, every ring is priced individually from the materials it needs and the manual shop time it takes. No surprises later.

Turned on the lathe

Your ring is cut, shaped, and sized by hand on a manual lathe. I’ll send progress photos along the way so nothing is a mystery.

Finished and shipped

Sanded, polished, sealed, and packed. It goes out to you with care notes for the specific materials in your band.

Materials

Chosen for the wearer, and for the planet.

I keep the material list deliberately small and deliberately responsible. Working on a manual lathe means offcuts are small, batches are single, and nothing gets made speculatively to sit in a bin.

  • Reclaimed and responsibly sourced stock wherever it exists
  • Eco-conscious resins and finishes
  • Comfort-turned interiors on every band
  • Made to order — no dead inventory, no overproduction

The maker

Hi, I’m Raeder.

I turn rings by hand in Salt Lake City. Not cast, not printed, not ordered from a catalog and engraved — cut on a manual lathe, one at a time, with my hands on the tool the whole way through.

That’s slower than the alternative, and it’s the entire point. It means your ring can be built around something that matters to you instead of something that happened to be in stock, and it means no two pieces that leave this shop are the same.

Questions

The things people ask first.

What does a ring cost?

It depends on the ring. Since every piece is one of one, pricing comes from the materials it calls for and the manual shop time it takes to turn. Send me your idea and I’ll come back with a specific quote before any work starts.

How long does a commission take?

Timelines move with the current queue and the complexity of the design. I’ll give you a realistic window with your quote, and I’ll tell you up front if a deadline isn’t something I can hit.

I don’t know my ring size. Can you help?

Yes. Hand-turned bands run differently from mall-store sizing, especially in wider profiles, so we’ll confirm your size before I cut anything. I’ll walk you through how to measure at home.

Can you work from a reference — a character, a game, a place?

That’s most of what I do. Bring me a fandom, a color palette, an album cover, a photo of a canyon — anything with a look you want to carry around. We’ll translate it into something wearable.

Can a hand-turned ring be resized later?

It depends on the materials and the construction, and I’ll be straight with you about that before you commit. Getting the size right the first time is always the better path, which is why we confirm it carefully.

Do you ship outside Utah?

Yes — the shop is in Salt Lake City, but commissions go out to wherever you are. Shipping is quoted with the ring.
